Data sources

Terrain & sea floor: Eusko Jaurlaritza / Gobierno Vasco, geoEuskadi — LiDAR 2017 MDT & isobaths (CC BY 4.0)

Boats in the port: digitised on the 2025 orthophoto, Eusko Jaurlaritza / Gobierno Vasco. geoEuskadi

Building heights: b5m, Gipuzkoako Foru Aldundia / Diputación Foral de Gipuzkoa (CC BY 4.0)

Buildings, streets & ground: Donostiako Udala / Ayuntamiento de San Sebastián, cartografía municipal 1:500 (CC BY-SA 3.0)

Neighbourhoods (auzoak): Donostiako Udala / Ayuntamiento de San Sebastián (CC BY-SA 3.0)

Street & place names, landmarks, water, bridges, street furniture: © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL)

The data was reprojected, simplified, resampled and combined for Slop Donostia; the derived data is under ODbL / CC BY-SA 3.0 (details).

Aitor Zabaleta's portrait on the tifo's banner at Anoeta: from a photo by Goiena.eus via Wikimedia Commons, in greys (CC BY-SA 4.0). The medallion it shows is the supporters’ work

Real time (Now): weather & sea data by Open-Meteo.com (CC BY 4.0), its forecast and marine models; the sun's and the moon's places after the Astronomical Almanac and Meeus' Astronomical Algorithms

Lettering: Ateburu, drawn for Slop Donostia from the Basque inscriptions in Louis Colas's La tombe basque (1923) (SIL OFL 1.1); fallback Donostia Display, Marcellus by Astigmatic with the crowned A (SIL OFL 1.1)

Code: MIT, forked from mrdoob/slop-newyork

Usage: a few events (the loading steps and their times, the frame rate and the quality, the viewpoints and the 100 m cells flown to, the switches used, the language, touch or mouse) go to PostHog Cloud EU. Without cookies unless you say yes: a daily server-side hash, counted, never recognised. With your yes, one first-party cookie, so the same visitor is the same person tomorrow. No ads, no identifiers, no recordings. Nothing is sent with Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control (details)
