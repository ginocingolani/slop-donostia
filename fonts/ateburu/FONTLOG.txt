FONTLOG for Ateburu
===================

This file provides detailed information on the Ateburu font software. This information should be distributed along
with the Ateburu fonts and any derivative works.


Basic Font Information
----------------------

Ateburu is a display typeface in the Basque lettering tradition (letra vasca), drawn from the inscriptions carved in
relief on the lintels, stelae and slabs of the Basque Country between the 16th and 19th centuries, chiefly as Louis
Colas drew them in La tombe basque (1923). It is heavy, wide and low in contrast, with short flared serifs. Its
Basque character rides on a few default letters: the crowned A (a flat bar over the apex and a crossbar dipped into
a V), the long-legged R, the curved K, the wedge-armed E and F, the spurred G, the open 4, the flat-topped 5, the
swan-necked 7 and the lozenge interpunct.

It is a capitals face: lowercase codepoints map to the same capitals. It has two variable axes: weight (wght), from
600 (SemiBold, the master tuned for light text on a dark panel at 12-20 px, with ink traps) to 900 (Black), and
curvature (CURV), from 0 (straight, the cut of 0.100) to 100 (stroke ends that flare, bracketed wedges, bowed
diagonals). Static instances: SemiBold, Bold, ExtraBold, Black, at CURV 0. The inscription forms (the dotted i, the
small o, the minuscules mixed among capitals, the nexus letters, Roman forms, plain figures and the curiosities of
the stones) are OpenType features.

The letters are drawn in code (source/*.py) and built with build.py, which is deterministic. The sources of each
identity form are listed in SOURCES.md. No existing font was traced or copied.

Ateburu is a contemporary interpretation of documented inscriptions, not "the authentic Basque letter": the idea of
one national Basque letter is a 20th-century construction (see README.md).

The name means "door lintel" in Basque (ate + buru). It is a working name.


ChangeLog
---------

2026-10-28 (The Ateburu Project Authors) Ateburu Version 0.200
- Curvature axis, CURV 0-100 (STAT: 0 Straight, 50 Carved, 100 Curved), from two more masters, HUD Curved and Black
  Curved: stems flare over their last third, wedge ends curve into their arms, T's arms droop and E F L Z bend a
  touch, A V W Y legs bow, X bends into an S, R and K legs run rounder into a longer flick. The variable font is now
  variable/Ateburu[CURV,wght].ttf (was Ateburu[wght].ttf); the statics stay at CURV 0.
- K redrawn: one curved Basque K with an open crotch, R's leg and R's flick. Two alternates: a straight-armed K
  (ss08, cv03 1) and the plain inscription K (ss04, cv03 2).
- Glyph set: all of GF Latin Core (accented capitals for the Western and Central European languages, Æ Ø Þ Ð Đ Ħ Ł
  Ŀ ẞ, combining accents, brackets, braces, @ # % * § ¶ © ® ™, currency, maths signs, guillemets, low quotes, the
  ellipsis and the bullet), Ꝑ (U+A750, U+A751), and ◌ (U+25CC) as a mark base. 350 glyphs.
- OpenType: mark, mkmk and ccmp for combining accents (stacking; a dotted i drops its dot under an accent); locl
  for Catalan (L·L to Ŀ L); calt (the A's crown turned right after T V W Y; shorter R and K legs before A T V W Y
  J); dlig nexus letters TX TZ TS TA TE TR NE ND ME DE LA AN MA; ss01 dotted i (with cv01 stem and cv02 dot);
  ss02 small o after L T K R; ss03 inscription unicase (e n m d t z r h q b); ss04 Roman A, R, K; ss05 plain 4 5 7;
  ss06 curiosities (8 for G, reversed N S D E, barred I, V for U); ss08 straight-armed K; tnum and pnum.
- 9 drawn on its own (no longer a turned 6); a wider Black A, so its counter stays open; a finer &; the Black C's
  aperture opened.
- Statics autohinted with ttfautohint; the variable font keeps no hints but has smart dropout control; the HUD
  subset is unhinted and now keeps to the letters and punctuation of place names (7.5 KB).
- Build: the masters are aligned run by run (dynamic programming over runs of segments on a common arc length), and
  the build fails on overlapping or wrongly turning contours anywhere on a wght x CURV grid, not only at the masters.
  Family-wide Windows metrics (944 / 296), one underline thickness, fsSelection REGULAR wherever nameID 2 is
  Regular, advances rounded once (so the maths signs share one width).
- The app's cuts, as Slop Donostia's owner chose them on the 0.200 playground (APP_SUBSETS in build.py): the HUD
  (Ateburu-HUD.woff2) is the SemiBold at CURV 50 and the titles (Ateburu-Title.woff2, new) the Bold at CURV 80, both
  with the dotted i baked in as a required feature (rclt): the short serifed stem with the round dot. The curved
  Basque K stays the default; no other feature is on.
- No case feature: the punctuation already sits on the capitals.

Known, left as they are in 0.200 (fontbakery check-googlefonts 0.12.10):
- No Regular at wght 400: the weight axis is 600-900 by design (the brief's 500-900 is not drawn).
- Version below 1.000.
- alt_caron: ď and ť (U+010F, U+0165) are drawn as Ď and Ť with the normal caron, as every lowercase codepoint
  shows its capital in this unicase face.
- Contour counts that differ from lowercase designs (the lowercase codepoints show capitals); the ink traps of
  K.straight and K.roman, reported as jaggy segments; on-curve points a unit or two off the baseline or cap line on
  curved terminals; a stem edge in ẞ 2 units off vertical.
- No avar table; vendor ID 'NONE'; no 'meta' table; the checks that need a METADATA.pb or the Google Fonts API.
- Soft outline notes in build-report.json: kinks under 9 degrees where curves meet at rounded points, 1-2 unit stubs
  on the curved masters' serif tips.

Planned:
- Arrows and ± for HUD use; the Latin-1 signs outside GF Latin Core (¤ ¦ ¬ µ, superiors, fractions).
- ss07 separators (U+2E31, ⁘, ⁙, a four-pointed star); Arbizu-style cycling of A, R and E variants as its own ss.
- The AR nexus (left out: the A's slanted leg makes no convincing R stem yet), HE, AL, MB.

2026-10-27 (The Ateburu Project Authors) Ateburu Version 0.100
- First cut, for review. Glyph set: A-Z (lowercase mapped to capitals), OE, 0-9, A E I O U acute, U dieresis,
  N tilde, C cedilla, A E O grave, the lozenge middle dot, . , : ; ! ? inverted ! ?, ' ’ ‘ " “ ”, hyphen, en and
  em dash, ( ) / &, space and no-break space.
- Two masters, HUD (600) and Black (900); variable font and four static instances; a subset WOFF2 for the Slop
  Donostia HUD.
- Spacing by an optical model and 785 kerning pairs, including A-T, T-A, L-T, R-A, R-T, Y-A, A-V, K-O and the
  Basque digraphs TX, TZ and TS.
