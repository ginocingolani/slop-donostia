Ateburu

A display face drawn from the carved inscriptions of the Basque Country: the lintels, stelae and slabs of the 16th to 19th centuries, as Louis Colas drew them in La tombe basque (1923). Heavy, wide, low in contrast, with short flared serifs; its Basque identity rides on a handful of letters. Variable weight 600–900. Version 0.100. SIL Open Font License 1.1, no Reserved Font Name.

Ateburu (Basque for a door lintel) is an original design: drawn in code from public-domain sources, chiefly Louis Colas's drawings of dated inscriptions, and from photographs of carved lintels; no existing font was traced or copied. See README.md, SOURCES.md and FONTLOG.txt. Copyright 2026 The Ateburu Project Authors. Licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, with no Reserved Font Name.

Why "letra vasca" is both old and new: the letterforms are on the stones; the idea of one national Basque letter is a 20th-century construction (Herrera 2004; Järlehed 2012). Ateburu is a contemporary reading of the inscriptions, not "the authentic Basque letter".
